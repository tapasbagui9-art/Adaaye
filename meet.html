The people (and one cat) behind it

It all starts here.

One artist, one studio cat keeping her company, and one small brand built by hand — this is where Adaaye begins, and where it still lives, one canvas at a time.

The Artist

Titas Basu

The hands behind every brushstroke — every Madhubani line, every hand-mixed colour, every signature on the back of a canvas.

The Studio Cat

Gupi Basu

Self-appointed quality inspector. Supervises every drying canvas from a safe distance and approves of absolutely none of them out loud.

The Studio

Adaaye

The name for all of it — Madhubani art, folk painting and hand-painted décor, made one small studio at a time. Artistically Indian.

A girl who never put the brush down, a cat named after her own family, and a studio that grew out of both. This is where Adaaye starts.
